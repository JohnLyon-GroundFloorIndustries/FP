ALLESHIA / OWNER & DESIGNER

A personal
point of view.

A Seattle native, Alleshia discovered floral design while creating the flowers for her own wedding. That experience continues to inform her work: the creative possibilities of flowers, and the importance of a thoughtful guide through the decisions.

Her approach is romantic and textural, with an instinct for movement and a close attention to the people a celebration brings together.

JADE / LEAD FLORAL DESIGNER

The rhythm of the season.

Based in Washington, Jade draws from the landscape and the changing seasons. Her work carries that natural sense of movement into organic, expressive compositions.
