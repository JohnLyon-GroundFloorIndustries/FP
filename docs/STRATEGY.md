# Strategy, evidence and measurement

## Positioning and information architecture
The creative brief targets substantial full-service celebrations. The redesign makes floral environment, regional scope and a consultation path explicit in the first screen. A deep green opening, burgundy process section, pale mineral reading surfaces and asymmetrical photography establish a consistent editorial system. Native fonts avoid download overhead. No invented awards, scarcity, minimum, client budgets or success statistics.

Public routes: /; /work; five /work/[slug] case studies; /studio; /approach; /commissions; /journal; three /journal/[slug] guides; /inquiry; /privacy; /accessibility; custom 404. Staff route /workspace is protected. Portal is not recommended for this phase per owner instruction; legacy endpoint is disabled. Legacy /about, /services, /portfolio, /contact and four journal-story URLs redirect permanently.

## Measurement contract
Optional events: service_view (/commissions), case_study_view (approved /work paths), inquiry_start, inquiry_submit_success and inquiry_error. Only fixed event name, allowlisted public path, service category, random event ID and server timestamp are stored. Query strings, names, email, dates and narratives are excluded. Consent is required; browser preference may be changed on Privacy. Success event fires only after server persistence. Metrics cover consenting interactions, so they are not a complete visitor denominator and can be affected by retries.

Protected operational records supply qualified leads, consultations, proposals, bookings, lost reasons, referral sources, first human response and administrative minutes. They are not sent to third-party analytics. Staff manually records stages and response evidence. Establish an initial 30-day baseline and reconcile duplicate/test records before selecting improvement targets. No conversion uplift, revenue projection or booking rate is claimed. Case-study engagement currently means page view; scroll-depth/dwell measurement is deliberately omitted to minimize scripts/data.
