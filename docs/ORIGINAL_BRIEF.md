Act as an elite digital strategy, luxury brand, UX, SEO, conversion, accessibility, and web-development agency. Fully redesign and rebuild the Flora Pacifica website at https://florapacifica.co/.

The result should feel like a bespoke website commissioned from a world-class creative agency for a floral design studio serving highly sophisticated weddings with floral investments commonly ranging from approximately $50,000 to $100,000.

Do not simply refresh the existing layout. Reconsider the positioning, information architecture, visual system, content, user journey, inquiry process, technical foundation, search visibility, and operational integrations.

## Primary objective

Position Flora Pacifica as a highly sought-after floral design atelier creating immersive floral environments for exceptional weddings and private celebrations.

The website must:

1. Attract clients, planners, venues, and creative partners aligned with Flora Pacifica’s artistic and financial scope.
2. Establish credibility immediately through exceptional work, informed point of view, and operational sophistication.
3. Prequalify inquiries without sounding transactional, defensive, arrogant, inaccessible, or focused only on price.
4. Communicate limited capacity through restraint, process, presentation, and language.
5. Encourage qualified prospects to request a private consultation.
6. Discourage poor-fit inquiries gracefully, without making visitors feel rejected or unwelcome.
7. Support organic search, local discovery, image search, referrals, planner relationships, and editorial opportunities.
8. Provide a fast, accessible, secure, responsive, and technically excellent experience.
9. Make the business easier to operate by improving intake, lead qualification, measurement, follow-up, and client handoff.
10. Preserve all truthful, valuable material from the current website while removing weak, obsolete, duplicated, misleading, or unrelated content.

## Positioning principle

Create an atmosphere in which access feels considered and the work feels scarce, without explicitly calling the company mysterious, restrictive, exclusive, elite, or difficult to book.

Do not say:

- “Not everyone is accepted.”
- “We only work with select clients.”
- “You must qualify.”
- “Exclusive clients only.”
- “For the elite.”
- “Luxury for those who can afford it.”
- “Application required.”
- “We choose our clients.”
- “Limited access.”
- “Mysterious” or “restrictive.”

Communicate discernment indirectly through:

- A considered number of annual commissions
- Private consultations after an initial review
- An inquiry form centered on place, intent, atmosphere, and creative alignment
- A confident creative process
- Full-service positioning
- Substantial visual case studies
- Restrained calls to action
- Deliberate pacing and generous white space
- Minimal promotional language
- Clear expectations around scope, collaboration, and investment
- A sense that every event receives meaningful studio attention
- Thoughtful response language for events that are not aligned

The visitor should naturally conclude that Flora Pacifica is in demand, highly intentional, and careful about the work it accepts.
