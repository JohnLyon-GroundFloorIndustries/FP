FULL-SERVICE FLORAL DESIGN

From a gesture
to an environment.

Floral direction, personal flowers, ceremony installations, reception flowers, and tablescape collaboration are considered together. The final proposal sets out the designs and production needed for your celebration.

INVESTMENT

Individually
considered.

Full-service wedding commissions are individually scoped. Guest count, venue, season, installation complexity, travel, production requirements, and creative direction all shape the investment.

Share your anticipated floral investment with your inquiry. It helps us understand the scale of the celebration and the possibilities within it.

Where does the studio work?

Across Washington and the Pacific Northwest. For celebrations further afield, share the destination so travel and production feasibility can be considered.

Can our planner begin the conversation?

Yes. Include the date, venue, floral priorities, and anticipated investment. The inquiry form welcomes introductions from couples and planning teams.

What happens after an inquiry?

The studio reviews the date, setting, and creative priorities before discussing a consultation. An inquiry does not reserve the date. Booking requirements are confirmed in your agreement.

Can we request specific flowers?

Tell us what you are drawn to. Flower choices depend on season and availability; the overall palette, form, and atmosphere guide the design.
