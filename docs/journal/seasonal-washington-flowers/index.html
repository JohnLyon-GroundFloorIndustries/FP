SEASON & PLACE

Flowers that feel at home in Washington

Plan around feeling, texture, and seasonal flexibility.

Let place lead the design

An orchard, a mountain view, a garden, and an urban venue each offer a different starting point. Look at the landscape, light, and spaces where guests will gather.

Plan a palette, not a shopping list

Share favorite tones and textures, then discuss flower choices with your florist. Seasonal flexibility helps a design remain cohesive when individual varieties change.

Prepare for outdoor logistics

Ask your venue about installation access, weather plans, shaded storage, and ceremony-to-reception transitions. Your florist and planner can help translate those constraints into a practical design.

Look at real regional work

Explore our Washington celebrations for garden-inspired, meadow-style, and mountain settings. Each wedding is its own design brief; examples are inspiration, not a promise of identical flowers.
