DESIGN & PRODUCTION

What a floral investment makes possible

The relationship between creative scope, setting, and the work behind an installation.

Start with the experience

An investment begins with priorities: the way a ceremony feels, the intimacy of a table, or the impression of entering a room. Identify the moments that matter before assigning flowers to every surface.

Understand production

A floral installation includes more than flowers. Design development, preparation, transport, labor, access equipment, installation, and removal can all shape the scope. Ask for these responsibilities to be clear in the proposal.

Let the setting inform scale

Guest count, ceiling height, sightlines, table layouts, and access windows influence what is possible. A change of venue or a second event space may require a different production plan.

Work from a shared brief

Bring your anticipated investment and creative priorities to the first conversation. A defined scope gives both client and studio a clear basis for design decisions.
