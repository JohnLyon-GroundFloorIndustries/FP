PLANNING GUIDE

A calmer start to wedding-flower planning

A practical checklist for your first conversation with a florist.

Begin with the moments that matter

Choose the places where flowers will make the most difference to you: the ceremony, your bouquet, shared tables, or a focal installation. You do not need a finished moodboard to start a conversation.

Gather a few useful details

Bring your date or season, venue or location, approximate guest count, floral budget, and any practical access or pickup constraints. A handful of reference images can help communicate feeling without prescribing every stem.

Give your budget a purpose

Decide which floral moments you would prioritize if scope needs to change. A proposal confirms the actual designs, quantities, logistics, and fees.

Leave room for the season

Describe the colors, textures, and feeling you love. Discuss availability and substitutions rather than assuming every flower is available in every month.

Ask about the whole day

Discuss delivery, installation, access windows, rentals, and who handles removal. Confirm responsibilities in your proposal and final plans.
